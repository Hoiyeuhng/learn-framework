# <Topic>: Errors and Misconceptions

Replace the topic name when creating a topic. Leave the record empty until an actual error occurs; the field descriptions below are not real errors.

Record fields:

- ID and date: A stable ID, such as M1, and YYYY-MM-DD.
- Related node: Its ID in `plan.md`.
- Question: Prompt, necessary conditions, and options, if any.
- My actual answer: Preserve the answer instead of replacing it with the tutor's explanation.
- Correction: Correct understanding and its necessary support.
- Diagnosis: Slip, isolated gap, misconception, or tentative; include diagnostic evidence.
- Hints: Independent answer, hints provided, or answer already shown.
- Status: Needs diagnosis, Needs repair, Needs review, or Passed review.
- Review history: Keep each date, variation, actual answer, hints, and result, including failed reviews.

Identify ambiguous questions and incorrect teaching as question or tutor issues, not learner misconceptions.
