# <Topic>: Errors and Misconceptions

Replace the topic name when creating a topic. Leave the record empty until an actual error occurs; the field descriptions below are not real errors.

Record fields:

- ID and date: A stable ID, such as M1, and YYYY-MM-DD.
- Related node: Its ID in `plan.md`.
- Context: Teaching, practice, or review; include the practice problem ID and mode when applicable.
- Question: Prompt, necessary conditions, and options, if any.
- My actual answer: Preserve the answer instead of replacing it with the tutor's explanation.
- Correction: Correct understanding and its necessary support, only after it has been explained. Write Pending while the correction remains undisclosed.
- Failure stage: Retrieval, discrimination, execution, conceptual, or undetermined; describe the observed step that failed.
- Diagnosis: Slip, isolated gap, misconception, or tentative; include diagnostic evidence.
- Assistance: Preserve exact hints and direct corrections, their order, and the highest level from None or H0-H5. Record if the answer was shown.
- Status: Needs diagnosis, Needs repair, Needs review, or Passed review.
- Retest and review history: Keep each date, new situation, actual answer, assistance, and result, including failures. Distinguish immediate retests from later reviews.

Identify ambiguous questions and incorrect teaching as question or tutor issues, not learner misconceptions.
Separate an observed failure stage from a tentative explanation of its cause. Do not erase assistance when a later attempt succeeds.
